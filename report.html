Report a Suspicious Offer

If a token offer asked for your password, pushed a download, or promised unlimited tokens, act quickly — then report it.

Immediate steps

  1. Stop and leave the suspicious site; do not enter anything else.
  2. If you shared a password, change it on the official platform immediately — and anywhere else you reused it.
  3. If you entered payment details, contact your payment provider and review your statements.
  4. If you installed software, run a reputable antivirus scan and remove unknown programs or extensions.
  5. Keep evidence: screenshots, URLs, and any messages you received.

Where to report

  • FTC — Report Fraud Official U.S. reporting portal for scams and fraud (ReportFraud.ftc.gov).
  • CISA — Recognize and Report Phishing How to report phishing messages and malicious sites.

You can also report abusive pages to your browser vendor (e.g., Google Safe Browsing) and to the platform being impersonated through its official support channels.
